The Project

An Address Inspired by Timeless Grandeur

Wide tree-lined avenues, a columned arrival portal and landscaped commons — planned as one development rather than sold as bare land.

A limited collection of 362 plots across 23 acres, part of Address Maker’s larger 90-acre township on the Nelamangala Growth Corridor: close enough to Peenya and the NICE Road network to stay connected, early enough in the corridor’s cycle to still enter at land-stage pricing.

The next chapter after Artemis Address, its second phase and Artemis Arena — Apollo Address continues the same vision under Address Maker, on the same trusted corridor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight Answers, From the Developer.

Apollo Address is sold directly by Address Maker. Everything below is verified project information — not a reseller’s summary of it.

Where exactly is Apollo Address located?

Apollo Address sits off Nelamangala Road, in the West-North Bengaluru Growth Corridor — close to Tumakuru Road, NICE Road and the Peenya industrial belt, roughly 10–12 minutes from NH 48 and the upcoming Nelamangala Metro Station.

What type of property is Apollo Address?

Apollo Address is a Greek-Themed Luxury Villa Plotted Development comprising 362 residential villa plots across 23 acres, located off Nelamangala Road.

How many plots are available, and in what sizes?

362 exclusive plots across four configurations:

30×40 (1,200 sq. ft.) — 102 plots
35×40 (1,400 sq. ft.) — 20 plots
30×50 (1,500 sq. ft.) — 140 plots
30×60 (1,800 sq. ft.) — 7 plots

What is the starting price?

From ₹58 Lakhs* for the 30×40 configuration. Larger configurations are price-on-request. *Indicative starting price, subject to availability and change; final pricing, taxes and registration charges are confirmed on the current price sheet from our sales team.

What amenities does Apollo Address offer?

Parthenon — The Club (pool, lounge and event spaces), a temple, putting green, party lawn and toddlers’ pool, plus Butterfly, Aroma and Herbal Gardens, a Senior Citizen Park, Mini Theatre, Conference Room, Barbecue Pavilion and Outdoor Cafe — set within landscaped commons and a central waterbody.

Is Apollo Address RERA registered?

Yes. K-RERA registration number PRM/KA/RERA/1250/307/PR/180826/008874. You can verify current project status on the official K-RERA portal at any time.

Can I schedule a site visit?

Yes — register through the form on this page or call our sales team directly. Because this is Address Maker’s own project microsite, your visit is scheduled straight with the developer’s sales desk, not routed through a reseller.

Is apolloaddress.com the official website?

Yes. This is the official project microsite, published and maintained by Address Maker, the developer. Other websites referencing Apollo Address may be run by third-party channel partners or listing portals and are not operated by Address Maker — always confirm pricing, availability and RERA details on this site or directly with our sales team before making a decision.
