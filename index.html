The Project

An Address Inspired by Timeless Grandeur

Wide tree-lined avenues, a columned arrival portal and landscaped commons — planned as one community rather than sold as bare land.

A limited collection of 210 plots on the Nelamangala Growth Corridor: close enough to Peenya and the NICE Road network to stay connected, early enough in the corridor’s cycle to still enter at land-stage pricing.

The next chapter after Artemis Address, its second phase and Artemis Arena — Apollo Address continues the same vision under Address Maker, on the same trusted corridor.
